# Model Card: Urban Resilience Multi-Target ML Predictor

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Predictor Role**: `predictor_role = "engineering_baseline"` (Engineering Pipeline Baseline).
> Predictor serves as an engineering pipeline baseline trained on synthetic simulator labels; empirical accuracy is not claimed.
> Empirical accuracy improvement or outperforming traditional baselines is strictly unverified and NOT claimed.

## Operational Modes & Deployment Status
The prediction subsystem supports two distinct operational modes:

### 1. Default Baseline Mode: `untrained_fallback`
- **Operational Mode**: `untrained_fallback`
- **Model Status**: `untrained_fallback` (engine: `monte_carlo_physics`). No pre-trained ML weights (`resilience_predictor.pt` or `resilience_predictor.joblib`) are assumed or bundled by default.
- **Provenance Contract**:
  - `summary_metric_source`: `"monte_carlo_physics"`
  - `curve_source`: `"monte_carlo_physics"`
  - `curve_metric_source`: `"monte_carlo_physics"`
  - `external_temporal_metric_source`: `"monte_carlo_physics"`
  - `metric_sources`: All 5 primary metrics mapped to `"monte_carlo_physics"`
  - `interval_source`: `"monte_carlo_quantile"`
- **Runtime Behavior**: In the absence of audited multi-event ground-truth training data, `SupervisorAgent` records `predictor_status={"model_status": "untrained_fallback", "engine": "monte_carlo_physics"}` in the `RunManifest`, explicitly executing the audited deterministic physics-based decay and Monte Carlo uncertainty simulator rather than claiming unvalidated ML inference.
- **Engineering Smoke Test Fixtures**: Fixtures and mock weights used in engineering tests (e.g. `tests/test_predictor_integration.py`) are strictly unit/integration verification harnesses testing serialization, schema validation, and fallback mechanisms. They are NOT empirical benchmark models.

### 2. Trained Artifact Mode: `trained_model`
- **Operational Mode**: `trained_model` (engine: `hist_gradient_boosting` / `scikit-learn`)
- **Provenance Contract & Role Division**:
  - `summary_metric_source`: `"trained_model"`. Disruption summary metrics (`maximum_activity_loss`, `recovery_time_hours`, `cumulative_activity_loss`) are predicted directly by the multi-target ML regressor.
  - `curve_source`: `"monte_carlo_physics"`. The high-resolution time-series trajectory curve `(time_hours, predicted_activity)` is generated by the physics-based Monte Carlo engine (`SynthesisAgent`).
  - `curve_metric_source`: `"monte_carlo_physics"`. Curve-derived indicators (e.g., `peak_loss_hour`, `resilience_triangle_area`) strictly originate from the Monte Carlo physics curve.
  - `external_temporal_metric_source`: `"monte_carlo_physics"`. Time-series validation metrics (RMSE, MAE, Pearson $r$, PICP) in `external_evaluator.py` strictly originate from the Monte Carlo trajectory curve, not the ML regressor.
  - `metric_sources`: Explicit mapping per metric:
    - `maximum_activity_loss`: `"trained_model"`
    - `recovery_time_hours`: `"trained_model"`
    - `cumulative_activity_loss`: `"trained_model"`
    - `peak_loss_hour`: `"monte_carlo_physics"`
    - `resilience_triangle_area`: `"monte_carlo_physics"`
  - `interval_source`: `"conformal"` (when non-conformity quantiles from validation events are calibrated) or `"uncalibrated"`.
- **Metric Source Separation Principle**:
  Macro-level disruption summary indicators (predicted by ML) and micro-level temporal dynamic curves (generated by physics Monte Carlo) must never be conflated. Reports, APIs, and CLI outputs strictly declare `metric_sources` and warn users of this architectural division.
- **Model Loading & Integrity Verification**:
  - Validates file existence and computes SHA-256 hash (`artifact_hash`).
  - Strict deserialization and validation of dictionary payload (`model_type`, `model_version == "1.0.0"`, `feature_schema_version == "1.0.0"`, `is_fitted == True`, and valid target estimators).
  - Corrupt, empty, or dummy text files are rejected with `ValueError` and logged as `untrained_fallback`.
- **Feature Assembly & Strict Schema (`FeatureAssembler`)**:
  - In strict mode (`strict=True`), all 22 domain and exposure features (including `drainage_capacity` and `hazard_intensity`) must be present with valid floats. Missing features or NaNs raise `ValueError` and trigger `insufficient_features` fallback status. Silent default fills (e.g., 0.5 or 50.0) are strictly forbidden.
- **Conformal Uncertainty Calibration (`ConformalIntervalCalibrator`)**:
  - Validates empirical non-conformity quantiles from hold-out validation events to produce guaranteed coverage prediction intervals $[q_{0.025}, q_{0.975}]$. When quantiles are unpopulated, intervals are marked `uncalibrated`.
- **Artifact Training Provenance & Synthetic Warning**:
  - Saved model artifacts embed strict `training_metadata` including `label_source`, `engineering_only`, `training_event_ids`, `calibration_event_ids`, and `holdout_metrics`.
  - When trained via `train_and_calibrate_synthetic_predictor`, the artifact is stamped with `label_source = "synthetic_simulator"` and `engineering_only = True`.
  - Supervisor, API, CLI, and external reports load and propagate these fields. When `engineering_only = True`, an explicit warning banner is rendered to alert users that the predictor is trained on synthetic simulator labels, not real historical disasters.
  - Legacy or untagged artifacts without metadata default to `training_label_source = "unknown"` and `engineering_only = False`, and are forbidden from posing as verified empirical models.

## Model Details
- **Architecture**: Multi-Target Gradient Boosted Trees (`HistGradientBoostingRegressor`) with Scikit-Learn backends (optional fallback to Ridge or Random Forest).
- **Version**: `1.0.0`
- **License**: Apache-2.0
- **Input Modality**: Tabular agent feature matrix (22 canonical features from 5 cognitive domains + physical exposure context).
- **Output Targets**:
  1. `target_maximum_activity_loss`: Peak percentage drop in population activity $[0.0, 1.0]$.
  2. `target_recovery_time_hours`: Elapsed hours from hazard onset until activity returns to within 5% of diurnal baseline $[1.0, 168.0]$.
  3. `target_cumulative_activity_loss`: Integrated volume loss across event disruption period.

## Data Leakage & Evaluation Protocol
1. **Event-Level Splitting (`EventLevelSplitter`)**: Folds are partitioned strictly on `event_id`. All zones and timestamps of an evaluation event are held out simultaneously. Zero temporal or spatial leakage into the training set.
2. **Ex-Ante Feature Extraction**: All agent feature inputs are extracted strictly from pre-event baseline observations ($T < T_{\text{event}}$). No post-shock mobility data is ever ingested into the feature pipeline.
3. **Conformal Uncertainty Calibration (`ConformalIntervalCalibrator`)**: Prediction intervals are calibrated using finite-sample split-conformal non-conformity quantiles on validation residuals, providing guaranteed nominal empirical coverage (e.g., 95% PICP).

## Synthetic Training & Multi-Seed Ablation Protocol (`engineering_only`)
1. **Synthetic Dataset Protocol (`build_synthetic_dataset`)**:
   - Training, calibration, and test datasets are generated by the physics-based urban simulation engine under strict event-level disjoint seed sets (e.g., train `[201, 202]`, calibration `[203]`, test `[204]`).
   - Every split is explicitly stamped with `label_source = "synthetic_simulator"` and `engineering_only = True`.
   - Disjointness assertion strictly verifies: `train_seeds ∩ cal_seeds = ∅`, `train_seeds ∩ test_seeds = ∅`, and `cal_seeds ∩ test_seeds = ∅`.
2. **Multi-Seed Ablation Experiments (`run_four_group_experiments`)**:
   - Evaluates four orchestration groups across multiple independent event seeds (`event_seeds: List[int] = [42, 43, 44]`):
     - `fixed_features`: Deterministic rule-based baseline without follow-up inquiries.
     - `single_domain_adaptive` (alias `single_adaptive`): Single-domain LLM reference (default: `temporal`), remaining 4 domains deterministic, follow-ups disabled. This is **not** a monolithic single-agent baseline; do not use it as such in comparisons.
     - `multi_uncoordinated`: Full multi-agent ReAct subagents across all 5 domains without supervisor review or follow-ups.
     - `multi_coordinated`: Full multi-agent orchestration with 2-round bounded review, cross-domain conflict arbitration, and follow-ups. When no localized cross-domain conflict is present, conflict resolution is reported as `N/A`, not as a vacuous 0%/100%.
   - Computes multi-seed statistical aggregations: sample mean ($\bar{x}$), sample standard deviation ($s$), and Student's $t$ 95% confidence intervals:
     $$\bar{x} \pm t_{n-1, 0.975} \cdot \frac{s}{\sqrt{n}}$$
   - All multi-seed experiment outputs (`experiment_summary.csv`, `experiment_raw_runs.csv`, `experiment_report.md`) are tagged `engineering_only = True`.
   - Follow-up task execution classifies `"refined"` outcomes as successful follow-up resolutions (`is_follow_up_success_status`), recording `coordination_status = "exercised"` when feature deltas occur and `followed_up_no_effect` otherwise.
3. **Empirical Status: BLOCKED**:
   - The synthetic dataset and multi-seed ablation pipeline verify end-to-end engineering, statistical aggregation, and predictor calibration contracts.
   - **Real-world empirical validation remains strictly BLOCKED** pending verified, open, unauthenticated municipal mobility telemetry for Shenzhen disaster events. No real-world scientific claim is made on synthetic results.
   - Calling `run_four_group_experiments(engineering_only=False)` or `run_experiment_group(engineering_only=False)` is strictly forbidden and raises `ValueError("Real empirical validation is BLOCKED: engineering_only=False requires a verified data loader.")`.

## Engineering Validation & Test Coverage

The predictor and experiment contract are validated as engineering infrastructure, not as an empirical accuracy claim.

1. **Audit metrics**:
   - `conflicts_detected` counts only localized `cross_domain` conflicts.
   - Domain anomalies and unlocalized conflicts are reported separately.
   - Metrics with no applicable denominator (no tool calls, no conflicts, no feature values) are `N/A/None`, never implicit 100%.
   - Fabricated feature values are rejected by the anti-fabrication gate and recorded in rejection counters.
2. **Conflict-targeted coordination**:
   - A controlled Supervisor integration test creates a localized `spatial`/`flexibility` conflict, delegates to the conflict zone, applies a refined follow-up, and verifies `conflicts_resolved > 0`.
   - Counterexamples verify that unrelated-zone follow-ups and anomaly-only runs do not increment resolved-conflict counts.
   - The default bundled synthetic event contains anomalies but no localized cross-domain conflict; its `conflict_resolution_rate` is therefore `N/A`. `coordination_status = "exercised"` in that case means information gain, not conflict resolution.
3. **Strict failure semantics**:
   - `allow_degraded=False` is tested with injected task failures and must raise rather than silently complete.
   - `allow_degraded=True` records degraded/failed status without masking it.
4. **Reproducibility and fixture integrity**:
   - `CachingLLMClient`/`ReplayLLMClient` support offline JSONL record/replay with strict missing-hash errors.
   - `FixtureEventSource.verify_source()` validates every checksum listed in `PROVENANCE.json`; tampering or missing files fail verification.
5. **Predictor scope**:
   - Synthetic training labels are simulator-derived and circular with the feature simulator; holdout metrics are pipeline diagnostics only.
   - Reports, API, and CLI expose `predictor_role = "engineering_baseline"` and synthetic-artifact warnings.
   - No empirical accuracy, baseline-superiority, or real-disaster generalization claim is supported.

## Limitations & Ethical Considerations
- **Data Dependency & Drainage Synthetic Attribution**: Model accuracy depends strictly on verified, audited pre-event mobility feeds. In the current Shenzhen dataset, `drainage_capacity` values ($30-65$ mm/h) in `spatial_loader.py` are engineering design assumptions derived from municipal drainage engineering standards (GB 50014; $30-40$ mm/h in older districts, $50-65$ mm/h in high-tech districts), NOT empirical IoT sensor telemetry. If real-world telemetry is unavailable, missing drainage values in strict mode safely trigger `insufficient_features`.
- **Engineering Only Flag**: All synthetic experiments and synthetic predictor weights are strictly marked `engineering_only = True` to prevent mistaking simulation verification for empirical scientific validation.
- **Out-of-Distribution Warning**: The model should not be extrapolated to hazard types or climatic magnitudes outside its historical training spectrum without prior recalibration.
